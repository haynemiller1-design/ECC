FAQ

Is my data really never uploaded?

Correct. Every tool runs with JavaScript in your own browser tab. Open your network inspector while compressing an image — you'll see zero requests. Your files and text are processed on your device and nowhere else.

Why is it so fast?

There is no server round-trip. Your CPU does the work directly, which for most tasks is faster than uploading a file, waiting in a queue, and downloading the result.

What exactly does Pro unlock?

Pro removes the single-item limits: batch image compression, bulk UUID and password generation, and one-click exports (color palettes, diffs, results). Free covers everything for occasional use; Pro is for people who use these tools as part of their job.

If everything runs locally, what am I paying for?

The engineering that makes a browser tab do the work of a dozen paid apps — and every future tool added to OmniKit. Lifetime means lifetime.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, yes — every tool keeps working with no connection at all.
