// why local-first

Your prompts are IP. Keep them.

Prompts encode how your product works. Most "prompt tools" upload them to a server you don't control.

Private by architecture

Every keystroke, token count and version stays in your browser's storage. Open the network tab — there's nothing to see. No account to breach, no vendor to trust.

One workflow, not ten tabs

Drafting, variables, token/cost math, versioning and diffing are the same job. PromptForge keeps them in one surface so you stop juggling a counter site, a notes app and a scratch file.

Yours to export

Your whole library is a JSON file you can download, commit to git, or move between machines. No lock-in, no "export unavailable on the free plan."

FAQ

Are the token counts exact?

They're fast, calibrated estimates — within a few percent for typical English. Exact counts require each provider's own tokenizer (Anthropic's count_tokens, OpenAI's tiktoken), which a tool that never uploads your prompt can't call. That's a deliberate trade: privacy over the last few percent. You can paste a known-exact count to calibrate your own estimates.

Where are my prompts stored?

In your browser's local storage, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Export your library to a JSON file anytime to back it up or move machines.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no login and no server. Pro unlocks with a license key stored locally. Accounts are optional and only ever used for cross-device sync, which is off by default.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Install it (there's an "Install app" button when your browser supports it) and the whole workbench runs with no connection.

What does Pro unlock?

An unlimited library with folders, uncapped version history, and bulk variable runs (fill a variable matrix and export every rendered prompt at once). Free is genuinely usable for daily work; Pro is for people whose job is prompts.
